## **T3Foundry — Brand Identity & Design Philosophy**

T3Foundry is the parent brand behind a family of identity products (DID Ninja, Verifive, TASRA and NumaQuest). Its look is calm, institutional and confidently European: a soft off-white canvas, one strong royal blue, deep navy for authority, and dark "midnight" sections that give the product portfolio a stage of its own.  
Where Tasra is sharp and geometric, T3Foundry is rounder and more open—large rounded cards, pill-shaped badges and buttons with softer corners. Blue carries the brand; each product's own color appears only inside that product's card.  
This guide reflects the live t3foundry.com website.

### **The Logo**

* **Mark:** An isometric cube built from interlocking "T" and "3" letterforms, in a blue-to-cyan gradient. On the website the mark always sits inside a white circle with a soft drop shadow.  
* **Wordmark:** "T3FOUNDRY" in an uppercase geometric sans, with a left-to-right gradient running from royal blue through navy to cyan.  
* **Descriptor:** "TRUST • TRANSPARENCY • TECHNOLOGY" in bold navy capitals, set beneath the wordmark.  
* **Name in running text:** **T3Foundry**: capital T, numeral 3, capital F, no space.

### **Messaging**

* **Headline:** "Europe's Sovereign Trust Infrastructure."  
* **Sub-headline:** "The commercial layer turning Europe's digital identity rollout into economic reality."  
* **Mission line:** "T3Foundry exists to make trust programmable — and the identity-native internet commercially viable."  
* **Footer tagline:** "Sovereign trust for a connected World."  
* **Voice:** British spelling (specialise, decentralised, monetisation). Short, declarative sentences. Spaced em dashes ( — ) to set off a clause.

## **1\. Color System Application**

Blue is the brand. Neutrals do the structural work, and the product colors are kept to their own cards.

### **The Neutrals (The Foundation)**

* **Page Background:** Cool Off-White (\#F8FAFD). Used across the page and behind the hero.  
* **Surface:** Pure White (\#FFFFFF). Used for the navigation bar, cards, the "Who We Are" section and the footer.  
* **Highlight Background:** Ice Blue (\#E8F2FF). Used behind callout statements.  
* **Gradient Tint:** Pale Blue (\#E0F0FF). Used for soft background gradients.  
* **Primary Text:** Near-Black (\#1A1A1A). Used for headings and body copy.  
* **Text Opacity Scale:** Near-Black at 75% (hero intro), 70% (subtle), 60% (card descriptions and muted copy) and 40% (faint).  
* **UI Gray:** Slate (\#4B5563). Used for navigation links, footer links and footer headings.  
* **Borders:** Light Gray (\#E5E7EB) for the navigation and footer dividers. Primary Blue at 5%, 10% or 20% for borders inside content.

### **Primary UI Colors (Trust & Action)**

* **Primary Blue:** Royal Blue (\#1F5EFF)  
  * *Usage:* Primary buttons, the navigation "Contact" button, the secondary-button outline, callout borders, the mission pull-quote, and the T3Foundry name where it is highlighted in copy.  
* **Dark Navy:** (\#102A83)  
  * *Usage:* The hero headline and the hover state of primary buttons. Navy is also defined as the color for a dark navigation bar variant.  
* **Secondary Blue:** Sky Blue (\#00A3FF)  
  * *Usage:* Section labels (eyebrows) on dark backgrounds, focus states on form fields, and the background glow in dark sections.  
* **Accent:** Light Cyan (\#5CC8FF)  
  * *Usage:* Text-selection highlight, paired with Dark Navy text.

### **Dark Sections**

* **Midnight:** (\#0B1121). Background of "The Trust Economy Stack" product section.  
* **Deep Navy Slate:** (\#151F36). Background of the "Let's Connect" contact section.  
* **Text on Dark:** White for headings, and white at 60% for supporting paragraphs.

### **Product Brand Colors**

Each product has its own color and a lighter companion. Use them only for that product: its card border, tagline, status badge and corner gradient.

* **DID Ninja:** Slate Gray (\#6B7280), light \#9CA3AF.  
* **Verifive:** Steel Blue (\#4A6FA5), light \#6B8FC5.  
* **TASRA (Flagship):** Teal (\#107595), light \#1491B8. This is the same teal used on the Tasra Network brand.  
* **NumaQuest:** Royal Purple (\#5B3FA6), light \#7B5FC6.

## **2\. Typography**

* **Primary Font (Headings & Body):** **Inter** in five weights: Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600) and Bold (700). Inter is the only typeface on the site. The wordmark is artwork, not live type.  
* **Styling Rules:**  
  * **H1 (Hero Headline):** Dark Navy (\#102A83), Bold (700), letter spacing \-0.025em, line height 1.25. 48px on mobile, 60px on desktop.  
  * **Hero Sub-headline:** Near-Black (\#1A1A1A), Bold (700), 20px.  
  * **Hero Intro:** Near-Black at 75%, Light (300), 16px on mobile and 18px on desktop, line height 1.6. The T3Foundry name is highlighted in Primary Blue.  
  * **H2 (Section Headings):** Near-Black (\#1A1A1A), or white on dark sections. Bold (700), 36px. The contact heading is 48px.  
  * **H3 (Card Titles):** Near-Black (\#1A1A1A), Bold (700), 24px.  
  * **Card Tagline:** Semi-Bold (600), 16px, in the product's own color.  
  * **Body Text:** Near-Black, Regular (400), 18px, line height 1.6. Inside cards: 16px at 60% opacity.  
  * **Section Label (Eyebrow):** Sky Blue (\#00A3FF), 12px, Bold (700), uppercase, letter spacing 0.2em (e.g., "CONTACT US").  
  * **Status Badges:** 12px, Bold (700), uppercase, letter spacing 0.05em (e.g., "COMING SOON", "IN DEVELOPMENT").  
  * **Navigation Links:** Slate (\#4B5563), Medium (500), 14px. Hover changes the color to Near-Black.  
  * **Footer Column Headings:** Slate (\#4B5563), 12px, Bold (700), uppercase, letter spacing 0.1em.  
  * **Form Labels:** White at 50%, 12px, Semi-Bold (600), uppercase, letter spacing 0.05em.

## **3\. UI Component Guide**

### **Navigation Bar**

* **Background:** White, fixed to the top of the page, with a 12px background blur and a 1px Light Gray (\#E5E7EB) bottom border.  
* **Brand:** The cube mark (inside a 48px white circle with a soft shadow) followed by the wordmark artwork.  
* **Links:** Home, Products, About, in Slate (\#4B5563).  
* **Contact Button:** A fully rounded pill in Primary Blue with white Semi-Bold 14px text, 10px vertical and 20px horizontal padding, and a soft shadow.  
* **Language Switcher:** EN / FR / DE in 12px Bold uppercase. The active language is Near-Black and the others are Gray (\#6B7280).

### **Buttons**

Buttons use a 6px border radius: softer than Tasra's 4px, but still clearly rectangular. Padding is 12px vertical and 28px horizontal, with 14px text.

* **Primary Button:**  
  * Background: Primary Blue (\#1F5EFF)  
  * Text: Pure White (\#FFFFFF), Medium (500)  
  * Hover State: Dark Navy (\#102A83) background.  
* **Secondary Button:**  
  * Background: White  
  * Border: 2px solid Primary Blue (\#1F5EFF)  
  * Text: Primary Blue, Semi-Bold (600), with a light drop shadow  
  * Hover State: Fills with Primary Blue, and the text turns white.  
* **Form Submit Button:** Full width, Primary Blue, white Bold uppercase text with 0.05em letter spacing, 4px radius and 16px vertical padding. It shows at 50% opacity while disabled.

### **Product Cards**

* **Container:** White, with a 2px border in the product color, a 16px border radius and 32px inner padding.  
* **Corner Shape:** A quarter-circle gradient (about 208px, with a 100px rounded lower-left corner) in the top-right corner. It runs from the product color at 40–50% opacity to its light companion at 15–20%.  
* **Product Logo:** Placed top-right, inside a 64px white circle with a soft shadow.  
* **Content Order:** Product name (H3), then the tagline in the product color, then a description at 60% opacity, then a status badge.  
* **Status Badge:** A pill in the product color on a 10% tint of the same color, with 4px vertical and 12px horizontal padding.  
* **Flagship Tag:** The flagship product (TASRA) carries a "FLAGSHIP" pill on its top-left edge, overlapping the border. Solid Teal with white text and 0.1em letter spacing.  
* **Hover:** A large, soft shadow fades in over 0.5s.

### **Callouts & Pull Quotes**

* **Callout:** Ice Blue (\#E8F2FF) background, a 4px Primary Blue left border, 8px rounded right corners, and 12px vertical and 24px horizontal padding. The key phrase is Bold Primary Blue (e.g., "That's what we build.").  
* **Mission Pull Quote:** Off-White panel, a 4px Primary Blue left border, 12px rounded right corners, 40px padding and a subtle shadow. Text is 24px Bold Italic Primary Blue.

### **Form Fields (on Dark Backgrounds)**

* **Field:** White at 10% fill, a 1px border in white at 20%, 4px radius, 12px vertical and 16px horizontal padding, white text. Placeholder text is white at 40%.  
* **Focus State:** Sky Blue (\#00A3FF) border with a 1px Sky Blue ring.

### **Sections & Footer**

* **Hero:** At least 95% of the viewport height, with 128px top and 80px bottom padding.  
* **Sections:** 96px of vertical padding (128px for the contact section).  
* **Footer:** White, with a 1px Light Gray top border and 64px of vertical padding. It holds the logo and tagline, the "Company" and "Connect" link columns, and the legal line (© entity · Switzerland · hello@t3foundry.com).

## **4\. Visual Motifs**

* **Swiss Hero Photography:** The hero uses a photograph of Geneva's lakefront, washed with white so the text reads cleanly. It fades into the Off-White page background through a 160px gradient at the bottom, and a small bouncing arrow (60% opacity) invites scrolling. It signals "Swiss-incorporated, European by design."  
* **Midnight Glow:** Dark sections use two large, heavily blurred circles of light (500–600px, 120px blur) in Primary Blue at 20% and Sky Blue at 10%, with a screen blend. A fine grain texture sits on top at 20% opacity.  
* **Rounded Corner Gradients:** The quarter-circle gradient in each product card's corner is the brand's signature shape. Reuse it in product color for product-specific material.  
* **Circular Logo Badges:** Logo marks (T3Foundry and each product) always sit inside white circles with soft shadows, never directly on a colored background.  
* **Restraint:** One blue family carries the brand. Product colors appear only in their own product's context and are never mixed into T3Foundry's own UI.
